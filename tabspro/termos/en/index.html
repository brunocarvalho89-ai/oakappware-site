1. What Tabs Pro does

Tabs Pro is an app to support guitar study that offers a chromatic tuner and the automatic generation of chord charts/tablatures from audio captured by the microphone or from files chosen by you. All processing happens on your device.

3. Automatic transcription: accuracy not guaranteed

Chord-chart generation uses automatic audio analysis. By nature, this process is approximate: chords, frets, tempo and rhythmic division may come out different from the original performance, and recordings with noise, several instruments or vocals reduce the quality of the result. The app offers correction tools (chord editing, transposition, tempo adjustment) precisely because the automatic result is a starting point, not an official score. OakAppWare does not guarantee the accuracy of the generated chord charts or their fitness for any specific purpose.
