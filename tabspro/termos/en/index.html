By nature, this process is approximate: chords, frets, tempo and rhythmic division may come out different from the original performance, and recordings with noise, several instruments or vocals reduce the quality of the result. The app offers correction tools (chord editing, transposition, tempo adjustment) precisely because the automatic result is a starting point, not an official score.
